Privacy policy — Receipts Organizer

Receipts Organizer is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • It reads the owner's own Google Calendar events and Gmail messages to list shifts, schedules and receipts, and it creates calendar events and email drafts in the owner's own accounts when the owner asks.
  • Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any other service.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
